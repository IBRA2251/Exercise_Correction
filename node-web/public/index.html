⚙ Trainer Studio

Record your own reps to train the AI baseline for any exercise

Exercise Setup

reps

More reps = better statistical model. 8–15 recommended.

  1. Select an exercise and set target reps
  2. Stand in front of your camera so your full body is visible
  3. Click Start Recording and perform the reps
  4. The system counts reps automatically — stop or let it auto-stop
  5. Review the stats, then Save Baseline to apply

How Baseline Training Works

📐
Joint Angles

6 key joints tracked: knees, elbows, hips. Min/max/avg/std computed per joint across all reps.

⚡
Movement Speed

Angular velocity (°/s) per joint. Coaches users who move too fast or too slow compared to baseline.

🎭
Motion Avatar

Every frame recorded as normalized landmark positions. Used for the demo replay overlay in coach mode.

🎯
ROM Analysis

Range of motion thresholds set from your baseline. Users coached on depth, extension, and alignment.
