Exercise Setup

reps

More reps = better model. 8–15 recommended.

  1. Select an exercise and set target reps
  2. Stand in front of camera so your full body is visible
  3. Click Start Recording and perform the reps
  4. System counts reps automatically
  5. Review stats, then Save Baseline

How Baseline Training Works

📐
Joint Angles

6 key joints tracked. Min/max/avg/std computed across all reps for accurate form detection.

⚡
Movement Speed

Angular velocity (°/s) per joint — coaches users who move too fast or too slow.

🎭
Motion Avatar

Every frame recorded as landmarks — used for the demo replay overlay in coaching mode.

🔬
Built-in Baselines

Research-based defaults from sports science literature are used until you record your own.
